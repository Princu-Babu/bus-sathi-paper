### 1.3 Deep-Dive Code Defect Analysis: `analysis/v04_gps_validation.py`

Direct line-by-line inspection of `analysis/v04_gps_validation.py` reveals eight technical, methodological, and mathematical defects that require a complete rewrite:

#### Defect 1: Truncation Bug and Missing Constraints in Fleet Calculation (Line 334)
- **Observed Code:**
  ```python
  fleet_obs = int(np.ceil(obs_cycle / headway) * C.PARAMETERS["FLEET_SPARE_RATIO"]["value"])
  ```
- **Arithmetic Bug:** Python's `int(...)` casts float to int via truncation (`floor`), NOT `ceil`.
  - On FDR-050: `obs_cycle = 110.1`, `headway = 20.0` $\rightarrow$ `np.ceil(110.1/20) = 6`.
  - `6 * 1.15 = 6.90`.
  - Line 334 evaluates `int(6.90) = 6`!
  - But the true engine rule is `math.ceil(operating * 1.15)`: `ceil(6.90) = 7`!
  - Similarly, FDR-262 ($6 \times 1.15 = 6.90 \rightarrow 6$ instead of $7$), FDR-370 ($4 \times 1.15 = 4.60 \rightarrow 4$ instead of $5$), and FDR-270 ($4 \times 1.15 = 4.60 \rightarrow 4$ instead of $5$) were each truncated by 1 bus.
  - This arithmetic bug alone generated the false finding of a fleet reduction from 27 to 23 ($-14.8\%$).
- **Missing Constraint:** The engine rule (`transit_kashmir_v3.py:3154`) enforces a minimum fleet:
  `fleet = max(max(1, ceil(operating * 1.15)), 1 if route_type == "Regional_District" else 2)`.
  The existing `v04` script omitted the route-type minimum fleet constraint entirely.

#### Defect 2: In-Sample Circularity of the 5 Matched Corridors
- The 5 `matched` corridors in `reality_check.csv` (C1, C4, C6, C7, C8) are the exact corridors whose GPS run times were used by engine v3.4.5 to re-anchor their cycle times.
- Evaluating cycle time or fleet error on these 5 corridors is an in-sample evaluation. The current script treated them as an independent validation of the model.
- STATUS.md and ORIGINAL_REQUEST.md demand that these 5 corridors be explicitly segregated and labeled `matched_in_sample`.

#### Defect 3: Conflation of Pre-v3.4.5 and Post-v3.4.5 Model States
- In `reality_check.csv`, `plan_oneway_min` is a pre-v3.4.5 snapshot of the engine before GPS corrections were applied.
- In `active` (`Rationalised_Routes_Kashmir_v3.csv`), `Cycle_Time_Min` and `Fleet_Required` represent post-v3.4.5 values.
- In line 341: `cycle_ratio = obs_cycle / float(r["Cycle_Time_Min"])`. This compares observed cycle against the post-v3.4.5 corrected cycle, while earlier sections compare `plan_oneway_min` against observed one-way time.
- The pre-correction cycle time for the 5 matched routes was bound by the per-km cycle cap:
  $$\text{cycle}_{\text{pre}} = \min(\text{plan\_oneway\_min} \times 2 \times 1.10, \text{eng\_km} \times 2 \times \text{cap})$$
  yielding: FDR-050: 76 min; FDR-262: 94 min; FDR-270: 74 min; FDR-370: 81 min; FDR-575: 25 min. The current script does not reconstruct or disclose this pre-correction state.

#### Defect 4: Invalid Whole-Route Length Comparison on Partial Matches (Lines 151–155)
- In `channel_length`:
  ```python
  summarise("Route length: plan Route_KM vs observed corridor", rc["obs_km"], rc["eng_km"], "km")
  ```
- Evaluated on all 14 rows in `reality_check.csv`.
- On `partial` matches, the observed corridor covers only a segment of the planned route (e.g. C11 has `obs_km = 11.2` while `eng_km = 23.2`; C9 has `obs_km = 7.4` while `eng_km = 23.2`).
- Evaluating route length MAPE across partial matches conflates matching scope limitations with route design error. Length comparison must be strictly restricted to the 5 `matched_in_sample` corridors.

#### Defect 5: Dwell Regression Lacks Run-Weighting (WLS) and Sensitivity to Outlier C17 (Lines 204–242)
- `channel_dwell` fits an unweighted OLS: `sm.OLS(d["dwell_min"], x).fit()`.
- The 18 corridor profiles vary widely in sample size: from 5 runs up to 211 runs.
- Corridor C17 is a severe low-run outlier: $n = 6$ runs, $\text{length} = 3.3\text{ km}$, $\text{dwell} = 31.8\text{ min}$, yielding a dwell rate of $9.64\text{ min/km}$ and dwell share of $74\%$.
- Unweighted OLS gives C17 equal weight to C1 (211 runs).
- Testing via `.venv` reveals:
  - OLS with C17: $\text{intercept} = 17.67$, $\text{slope} = 0.247$, $R^2 = 0.030$.
  - OLS without C17: $\text{intercept} = 13.49$, $\text{slope} = 0.506$, $R^2 = 0.120$ (slope doubles).
  - WLS with C17 (weighted by `n_runs`): $\text{intercept} = 20.72$, $\text{slope} = 0.196$, $R^2 = 0.019$.
  - WLS without C17: $\text{intercept} = 20.24$, $\text{slope} = 0.225$, $R^2 = 0.024$.
  WLS is robust to C17 because of its small weight, but both models must be formally reported alongside C17's influence.

#### Defect 6: Misattribution of Congestion Speed Counterfactuals (Lines 187–202)
- `channel_moving_speed` tests OSRM free-flow speed divided by 2.2 (City_Core), 1.4 (Peri_Urban), and 1.0 (Rural) against observed `moving_kmh` across all 18 corridors.
- All 18 observed corridors are located in the Srinagar urban/peri-urban zone.
- Presenting the 1.4 and 1.0 tests as evaluations of rural or peri-urban speed models is invalid; they are classification counterfactuals showing what would happen if those multipliers were misapplied in the core.

#### Defect 7: Cycle Cap Census Omitted by Route Class (Lines 244–286)
- `channel_cap` reports overall routes at cap, but fails to report the breakdown by route class across all 186 active routes.
- Testing across the 186 active routes in `PLAN_CSV` demonstrates:
  - `Regional_District`: 71 / 71 routes ($100.0\%$) sit exactly at the 1.5 min/km cap.
  - `Peri_Urban`: 46 / 47 routes ($97.9\%$) sit exactly at the 2.5 min/km cap.
  - `Urban`: 52 / 68 routes ($76.5\%$) sit exactly at the 4.0 min/km cap.
  - Overall: 169 / 186 routes ($90.9\%$) sit at the cap.
  - Above cap: exactly 5 routes ($2.7\%$) — which are precisely the 5 GPS-corrected routes!
  - Below cap: exactly 12 routes ($6.5\%$), all Urban.
- This proves that a guard clause (sanity cap), rather than the unconstrained model, became the binding constraint determining the published headline fleet. This finding (F10) must be promoted to a primary headline in `v04`.

#### Defect 8: Omission of `permit_observed.csv` Service Status (Finding F12)
- `channel_route_coverage` only inspects `route_evidence.csv` (`obs_frac`).
- It ignores `permit_observed.csv`, which documents `status` (OBSERVED 66, PARTIAL 45, NO_APP_DATA 75) and `observed_cover`.
- The two metrics measure distinct operational properties:
  - `obs_frac`: spatial coverage of roads by bus GPS runs (corroborates road network alignment).
  - `observed_cover / status`: coverage of planned service by recurring transit trips (corroborates service recurrence).
  Both must be reported with their source-code definitions.
