## Proposed co-author cuts — for the section owners to accept, amend or reject

Nothing below has been applied. Co-author prose is untouched in the section files.

1. **§1 "Policy Failure" block (897 → ~350, −550).** It is institutional context, not introduction
   (FLAG PF-a). Move it to §3.3 "Existing supply and institutional structure", keeping the three facts a
   reader needs — the permit regime under the Motor Vehicles Act 1988, the absence of any
   origin–destination or boarding data, and the overlap of RTA/SSCL/municipal responsibilities — and drop
   the narrative history. It also carries the barred "Srinagar Municipal" scope (D3), which the move is the
   moment to fix.
2. **§1.1–§1.4 (917 → ~650, −270).** §1.1 and §1.2 both argue that bus reform matters in Indian cities;
   merge them into one paragraph. §1.4 repeats the data-scarcity argument of §1.3; keep one.
3. **§7 "Policy Contribution" block (429 → ~150, −280).** Its content duplicates §7.2 (instruments for
   the State Transport Authority). Fold the non-duplicated points into §7.2.
4. **§7.8 Limitations (265 → ~120, −145).** §6.4 and §4.10 already state the demand-side and supply-side
   limits; §7.8 should list only what those do not (transferability, the 2011 census frame, political
   feasibility).
5. **§3.5 Data-quality diagnostics (419 → ~250, −170).** Several diagnostics are restated in §4.2
   (permit→corridor, WorldPop vs Census). Keep them in one place; §4.2 is the natural home because the
   equations refer to them. Prashant can shorten §4.2 instead if the co-author prefers to keep §3.5.

Sum of proposals 1–5 ≈ −1,415; with the placement notes, ≈ −1,755. Prashant's own trims (§6 −317,
